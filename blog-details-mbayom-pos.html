A Smarter, Simpler Way to Manage Sales, Stock & Business Operations

Running a business should not mean struggling to keep track of sales, inventory, customers, payments, and daily operational activities.

That is why Mbayom IT-Point Global Solutions is preparing to launch Mbayom POS — a modern point-of-sale and business management application designed to help businesses take better control of their daily operations.

Mbayom POS is a modern business management solution designed for businesses that need more than just a system for recording sales.

What Is Mbayom POS?

Mbayom POS brings essential business processes together in one simple, easy-to-use platform, helping owners and staff manage transactions, products, inventory, customers, payments, reports, and more.

Whether you run a retail shop, supermarket, fashion store, cosmetics business, pharmacy, restaurant, or another SME, Mbayom POS is being built with real-world business needs in mind.

Manage Your Products & Inventory

Keeping stock manually can lead to errors, losses, and uncertainty about what is available. Mbayom POS makes it easier to:

  • Manage products and categories
  • Track stock levels
  • Monitor low-stock items
  • Search products quickly
  • Use barcode scanning for faster sales
  • Record purchases and stock movements
  • Monitor inventory across business locations
  • Keep better records of your products

Faster & Easier Sales

Mbayom POS is designed to make the sales process simple and efficient. Staff can quickly find products, scan barcodes, add items to a transaction, choose payment methods, and complete sales without unnecessary complications.

The system also supports different payment scenarios, including cash, electronic payments, and customer account transactions.

Customers & Customer Accounts

Your customers are an important part of your business. Mbayom POS helps you maintain customer records and track transactions, including customers who buy on credit or make payments toward outstanding balances.

This creates better visibility into customer accounts and outstanding payments.

Understand Your Business With Reports

Good business decisions require good information. Mbayom POS provides access to important information such as:

  • Sales records
  • Profit information
  • Inventory levels
  • Low-stock products
  • Customer transactions
  • Payment records
  • Purchase history
  • Business activity
  • Top-selling products

Instead of relying on notebooks, scattered records, or manual calculations, business owners can have their important information organized in one system.

Built With Growing Businesses in Mind

Mbayom POS is being developed with scalability in mind. Businesses with multiple locations can manage their operations while keeping track of where transactions and inventory movements take place.

This makes the system suitable not only for small businesses but also for growing businesses that need better control as they expand.

Designed for Business Control

Different team members often have different responsibilities. Mbayom POS includes user management and permissions so businesses can control what different users can access within the system.

Business owners and administrators can maintain better visibility over activities carried out by staff.

Built by Mbayom IT-Point Global Solutions

Mbayom POS is being developed by Mbayom IT-Point Global Solutions, a technology solutions company focused on helping businesses improve operations through practical technology.

Our goal is simple: to help businesses stop losing track of their sales, stock, customers, and business activities.

We understand that every business is different. That is why Mbayom POS is being developed with flexibility and customization in mind.
